How to read the map

  • Orange lines lead from a course back to what it builds on; blue lines lead forward to what it unlocks.
  • Dashed related links mark courses that connect without being required.
  • A tick on a course means you have read most of that page on this device. The record stays in your browser only, and can be cleared with the link in the footer.
