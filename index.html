A university curriculum in computational materials science

Start with first-year calculus, linear algebra, physics and materials science, build up through differential equations, probability, quantum and statistical mechanics, and arrive at the methods researchers use: density functional theory, molecular dynamics, Monte Carlo, phase-field, finite elements and machine learning. Every course teaches its concepts from the beginning, with worked examples and problems that have full solutions.

About

MiBeM is a free, self-contained curriculum for anyone who wants to learn computational materials science properly: undergraduates, graduate students, and researchers or engineers moving into the field. It is organised like a university programme, with lecture-style courses, worked examples, problem sets with solutions and unit quizzes, and it teaches the mathematics and physics the methods depend on rather than assuming them.

The animated figures and interactive demos come from small real simulations or computed curves, so what they show is what the method does. Journal details in the reference lists should be checked against the originals before you cite them.
