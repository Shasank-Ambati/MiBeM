About

MiBeM is written for anyone who studies, makes or tests materials: students, researchers and engineers working with metals, ceramics, polymers, semiconductors or composites. Pages assume a materials-science background (thermodynamics, crystal structure, mechanical behaviour) but not prior experience with simulation software.

The animated figures come from small real simulations or computed curves, so what they show is what the method does. Journal details in the reference lists should be checked against the originals before you cite them.
