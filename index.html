About

MiBeM is written for anyone who studies, makes or tests materials: students, researchers and engineers working with metals, ceramics, polymers, semiconductors or composites. The background pages refresh the mathematics, physics, materials science, numerical methods and programming that the core topics rely on, so no prior experience with simulation software is assumed.

The animated figures come from small real simulations or computed curves, so what they show is what the method does. Journal details in the reference lists should be checked against the originals before you cite them.
